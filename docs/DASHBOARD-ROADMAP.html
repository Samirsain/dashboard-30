Technology & Scaling Plan

Prepared for Management · Decision Document · July 2026

Hamara MIS Dashboard live hai — Google Sheets ke data se poori company ka kaam (Task List, Checklist, multiple offices) track hota hai. Data hamesha Google Sheet me hi rahega (safe & in our control). Ye document 3 options deta hai taaki jaise-jaise company aur sheets badhein (aaj 3 → kal 50+), system utni hi speed & reliability se chale.

1. Abhi kya ready hai (delivered)

  • Executive Dashboard — KPIs, department performance, status breakdown, sabki ek nazar me progress.
  • Task List & Checklist — seedha Google Sheets se live, har doer ka kaam.
  • Done / Revise — dashboard se hi task complete ya reschedule; wapas Google Sheet me likhta hai.
  • Multi-Sheet — Admin panel se nayi Google Sheets easily connect (alag office / kaam ki alag list).
  • Access control — konsi sheet kis doer ko dikhe, admin decide karta hai.
  • Cross-device — assignment ek laptop pe karo, har device pe same dikhta hai.
  • Clean view — sirf pending kaam dikhta hai; complete hote hi hat jaata hai (record Sheet me rehta hai).
  • Speed — caching (server + browser) se repeat loads fast.
  • Login & roles — Admin / PC / Employee accounts.

2. Core principle — Data Google Sheet me hi

Poora system is tarah banaya gaya hai ki asli data Google Sheets me rahe — kyunki wahan data safe hai, backup hota hai, aur team usse seedhe bhi kholker dekh/edit kar sakti hai. Neeche ke saare options is soch ka samman karte hain. Farak sirf itna hai ki data ko kitni tezi se aur kitne bade scale par serve kiya jaaye.

3. Aage ki soch (scaling ka sawaal)

Aaj 2–3 sheets hain — sab theek. Jab 50+ sheets aur zyada log ek saath use karenge, tab "sheet ko baar-baar padhne" wala current tareeqa dheema pad sakta hai. Iske 3 raaste hain:

Option A  Abhi wala

Kaise: Google Sheet + Apps Script (+ caching, jo add kar diya gaya hai).
Speed: Theek (cache se), par bhaari load par dheema.
50 sheets: Apps Script ki limits (execution time, daily quota) aड़चन ban sakti hain.
Data: Google Sheet me.   Kharcha: Free.   Mehnat: Ban chuka.

Faisla: Aaj ke chhote scale ke liye badhiya. Badi company ke liye long-term nahi.

Option B  Recommended

Kaise: Google Sheet (data wahi) + official Google Sheets API + Vercel serverless + ek cache layer. Apps Script hata dete hain.
Speed: Kaafi fast — koi cold start nahi, koi 6-min limit nahi.
50 sheets: Cache layer load & API-limits dono sambhaal leta hai.
Data: Google Sheet me hi (100% same).   Auth: Proper login.   Kharcha: Free/sasta.
Mehnat: Medium (one-time setup).

Faisla: Data Sheets me rakhte hue sabse behtar balance — speed, scale & kam kharcha. Growth ke liye yahi.

Option C  Enterprise

Kaise: Asli database (Supabase / Postgres) + Google Sheet ko auto-backup/export.
Speed: Instant, lakhon rows par bhi.   50/500 sheets: koi farak nahi.
Data: Database me (Sheet me auto-copy backup ke liye).   Auth: Best (row-level security).
Kharcha: Free tier bada, sasta.   Mehnat: Zyada (migration).

Faisla: Jab company bahut badi ho jaaye. Sabse fast & scalable, par tab data ka "asli ghar" database ban jaata hai (Sheet backup rahता hai).

5. Recommendation (phased plan)

Abhi (0–3 mahine): Option A — jo ban chuka hai. Current scale ke liye kaafi. Kharcha zero.

Growth phase (jab sheets/users badhein): Option B par shift — data Google Sheet me hi rakhte hue speed & scale mil jaayega. Yehi hamari sifarish hai.

Enterprise (bahut badi company): Option C — database, Sheet auto-backup ke saath. Sirf tab jab data/users bahut zyada ho jaayein.

Ek line me: Data hamesha Google Sheet me safe rahega. Jab scale badhe, Option B se system 50+ sheets par bhi tez & reliable chalega — kam kharche me.

6. Kharcha (summary)

Teeno options free ya bahut kam kharche me chalte hain: Vercel — hosting (free/low) Google Sheets — free Sheets API — free Supabase — free tier
Koi bhi bada server ya license kharcha nahi. Scale badhne par hi mamooli cost aati hai.

  • Ya abhi Option A par hi rahein aur baad me review karein?
